Generate Stock Requisitions
Creates one requisition per line from the Production Schedule's still-scheduled jobs for a date — jobs already in progress or completed are left out, and a line that already has a requisition for that date is left alone rather than overwritten. Raw materials that aren't batch-tracked are reduced by what's already at the line.
Create a requisition
An empty requisition for one line, to fill in by hand. Use it for a top-up or anything not on the schedule. A line that already has a requisition for the date gets the next number, e.g. "(2)".
Send to Unleashed
Every requisition marked Completed for that date becomes a Parked Warehouse Transfer in Unleashed, from PRD to its line, with each batch number on its line. Complete it in Unleashed once the stock has moved — that is what makes the batches available to the line's assemblies.
Raw Material Balance
For raw materials that aren't batch-tracked in Unleashed: how much has been sent to each line by completed requisitions, versus how much the Production Schedule has recorded as used (including wastage). Always current — the balance carries forward into the next requisition.
